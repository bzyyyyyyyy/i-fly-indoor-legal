Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for I Fly Indoor

Effective date: 2026-06-01

I Fly Indoor ("we", "us", or "our") provides an indoor flight logging application that allows users to record flights, manage model types, and export their own data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we share it, and what choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address
  • User ID
  • Authentication provider information, such as Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple

Profile Information

  • Display name
  • Profile avatar or avatar image upload

User Content

  • Model types
  • Flight records
  • Flight-related notes, telemetry, settings, and other data you enter
  • Exported content you choose to generate from your own records

Feedback and Support Information

  • The content of your feedback
  • Your contact email, if you provide it

Technical and Usage Information

  • IP address
  • Device and platform information
  • Authentication and session metadata
  • Error or service logs needed to maintain app functionality and security

2. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate you and keep your session active
  • Store and sync your data across supported devices
  • Display your profile and avatar
  • Let you create, edit, view, and export your own flight data
  • Respond to feedback and support requests
  • Maintain security, reliability, and abuse prevention
  • Improve the app and prepare future features

3. How Your Information Is Stored

Your account and app data may be stored locally on your device and remotely through our backend service providers.

Local storage may include cached app data, offline data, and temporary files needed for app functionality, such as avatar cache and synced records.

Remote storage may include account data, profile information, flight records, model types, and related app content that you choose to store through the service.

4. Service Providers

We use third-party service providers to operate the app.

  • Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and backend functions
  • PowerSync for synchronization and offline data sync infrastructure
  • Google and Apple for authentication, where you choose those sign-in methods
  • Google Forms or a similar external form service for feedback collection, if you choose to submit feedback

If we enable subscription or billing features in the future, we may use an additional billing provider and update this Privacy Policy as needed.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With service providers that help us operate the app
  • When required by law, regulation, legal process, or government request
  • To protect the rights, safety, and security of users, our services, or others
  • In connection with a business transfer such as a merger, acquisition, or asset sale

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the app and operate the service, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

If you delete your account, we will delete your account and associated app data, including your profile information, model types, flights, and avatar uploads, subject to any limited retention required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or operational purposes.

7. Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account from within the app.

In the current app structure, account deletion is available through:

  • Settings
  • Account Security
  • Delete Account

Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone.

8. Your Choices

  • Not create an account
  • Stop using the app
  • Update your profile information
  • Remove your avatar
  • Delete your account from within the app
  • Contact us regarding privacy or support questions

9. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. International Data Processing

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, depending on where our service providers operate their infrastructure.

11. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to help protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we may update the effective date above and provide notice through the app or by other appropriate means.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our handling of your information, contact us at:

  • Email: support@iflyindoor.com
  • Support URL: https://legal.iflyindoor.com/global/support.html
