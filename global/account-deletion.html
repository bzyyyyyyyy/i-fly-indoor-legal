Account Deletion

Delete Your I Fly Indoor Account

You can delete your account directly inside the app. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open Settings
  2. Open Account Security
  3. Select Delete Account
  4. Confirm the deletion request

What Will Be Deleted

  • Your account record
  • Your profile information
  • Your model types
  • Your flight records
  • Your uploaded avatar image

What May Be Retained

We may retain limited information where reasonably necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or operational purposes.
